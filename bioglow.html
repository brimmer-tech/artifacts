Nanoscale Membrane & Electrostatic Physics

Electrostatic Shielding Active
Ca²⁺ Divalent Cations pBioGlow Plasmid Outer Membrane (LPS) Heat Shock Pore

Bozeman & Henrik's Lab Principle: Bacterial cells and plasmid DNA both carry high negative surface charges. Divalent Ca²⁺ creates electrostatic salt bridges that allow DNA to adhere to the membrane. A thermal surge to 42°C creates transient thermal convection pores, drawing plasmids inward.

Transcriptional Operon Regulation

State: Repressed (OFF)

In pBioGlow / pGLO vectors, transcription of the eGFP gene is under an inducible operon. When the repressor protein is bound to the operator, RNA Polymerase is physically blocked.

Active repressor is bound directly to the operator. RNA Polymerase is physically blocked from transcribing eGFP.
Key AP Biology Distinction:

Constitutive vs. Inducible: The bla gene confers ampicillin resistance constitutively (always active). The gfp gene is inducible, producing fluorescence only when an allosteric effector binds the repressor.
