    How it works

    Each face of the cube is one drum. The eight squares around the edge are eight steps, played clockwise starting from the top-left corner. The center square names the drum; tap it to mute or unmute that drum. In the grid, tap a drum’s name to hear it, M to mute it, or S to hear it on its own.

    Turning a face carries lit squares onto neighboring faces, so they land on a different drum and a different step, and take on that drum’s color. That’s the whole idea: the puzzle cube becomes a source of musical randomness, like the dice games composers once used to write music by chance.

    Shuffle turns a random face at the end of every bar. Solve puts every square back where it started, and your original beat comes back with it.

    Keyboard: press U, D, L, R, F or B to turn a face clockwise, add Shift to turn it the other way, and press Space to start or stop.

    Drum sounds are synthesized live in your browser.
