    How it works

    Each face of the cube is one drum. The eight squares around the edge are numbered 1–8 clockwise starting from the top-left downbeat corner (or 9–16 on the second half of a 16-step bar). Tap a square to cycle Off → Normal → Accent, or Shift-click to toggle an Accent directly. The center square names the drum; tap it to mute or unmute that drum. In the grid, tap a drum’s name to hear it, M to mute it, or S to hear it on its own.

    Turning a face carries lit squares onto neighboring faces, so they land on a different drum and a different step, and take on that drum’s color. Swipe any square on the cube to twist a face or a middle slice, or use the arrow buttons. A middle slice carries lit squares across four faces and takes the center drum labels with it. A label brings its sound, color, mute, solo and volume to its new face, so a face always plays the drum whose label is on it.

    With Steps set to 16, the bar has sixteen faster steps. The cube and grid show half the bar at a time; leave Follow on to watch it flip automatically, or switch halves manually with “Steps 1–8” and “Steps 9–16”. Swing delays every other step for a looser feel.

    Shuffle turns a random face or middle slice near the end of every bar. Solve puts every square back where it started, and your original beat comes back with it. Undo steps back through your last changes.

    Keyboard: press U, D, L, R, F or B to turn a face clockwise, M, E or S for a middle slice (M sits between L and R, E between U and D, S between F and B), add Shift to turn the other way, press Space to start or stop from anywhere, and Ctrl or Command with Z to undo.

    Drum sounds are synthesized live in your browser.
