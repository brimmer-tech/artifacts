How to use this simulation

  1. Look at the starting dish

    Blue dots are healthy people who could get sick. A few pink dots are already infected. Purple dots (if any) are vaccinated.

  2. Press Start

    People begin wandering. When a pink dot comes within the contact distance of a blue dot, there's a chance the blue dot becomes infected. Each tick of the counter in the corner is one day.

  3. Watch the chart and counts

    The chart stacks every group over time. The pink band is the "curve." Note the peak number infected and the day it happened.

  4. Let it finish

    The simulation stops on its own when nobody is infected anymore. Record the final numbers: peak infected, peak day, and the percent ever infected.

  5. Change one setting

    Move exactly one slider, such as Vaccinated or Staying home. Starting settings reset the dish for you; otherwise press Reset to begin a fresh run.

  6. Run it again and compare

    Did the peak get lower? Later? Did fewer people get sick overall? Write down what changed and why you think it happened.

  7. Repeat to check your result

    Randomness means two runs with the same settings won't match exactly. Run each setting 3 times and average the results before drawing a conclusion.

  8. Use Step 1 day to look closely

    Pause and press Step 1 day to advance slowly and watch individual infections happen.
